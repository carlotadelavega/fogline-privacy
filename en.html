Fogline Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 30, 2026

This policy explains how Fogline (the “app”), developed by Meridia (“we”), handles the information of people who use it. Fogline is a logic puzzle game with no user accounts, no first-party analytics and no real-money purchases.

1. Controller and contact

Data controller: Meridia. For any privacy question, contact us at labsmeridia@gmail.com.

2. Information stored on your device

The app stores only on your device the data needed to play: selected language, campaign progress, completed daily challenges, coins and items, settings (such as haptics), and tutorial and in-app shop data (such as the day of your last reward). This data is not sent to our servers —we have none— and is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data in your system settings.

3. Information we do not collect

  • We do not ask you to register, or for your name, email or phone number.
  • We do not access your location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera.
  • We do not use our own analytics or crash-reporting tools.
  • We do not sell personal data.

4. Ads (Google AdMob)

The app shows optional rewarded ads (for example, to earn coins) through Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC. When an ad is loaded or shown, AdMob may automatically collect and process, as an independent controller or processor depending on the case:

  • device identifiers such as the Android Advertising ID;
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it;
  • device and app information (model, OS version, language, app version);
  • ad interaction data (impressions, clicks, completions).

This data is used to serve and measure ads, prevent fraud and, if you allow it, personalize them. You can reset or delete your advertising ID and turn off personalization in Android Settings › Google › Ads. Learn more: how Google uses data from advertising and Google’s Privacy Policy.

5. Users in the European Economic Area and the UK

Where required by law, processing for advertising purposes is based on your consent, which you can manage or withdraw at any time through the app’s consent message, when available, or your device’s ad settings. Data may be transferred outside the EEA (for example, to the United States) under the safeguards applied by Google.

6. Your rights

Under the GDPR and applicable law, you may exercise your rights of access, rectification, erasure, objection, restriction and portability, and withdraw your consent, by writing to labsmeridia@gmail.com. Since we do not keep your personal data on our systems, in practice you control game data on your device, and advertising data is handled by Google. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your data protection authority (in Spain, the AEPD).

7. Children

Fogline is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has provided us with data, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Security and retention

Game data stays in the app’s local storage, protected by the Android system. We do not retain personal data on our own servers.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy; the date above shows the latest version. Material changes will be posted at this same address.
